About The Net Effect

The Net Effect is a basketball blog built around a simple idea: some of the most interesting decisions in the NBA make a lot more sense when you look at the incentives behind them.

What is The Net Effect?

This site is where I explore basketball through economics, game theory, analytics, roster construction, salary-cap strategy, and whatever other questions around the NBA happen to interest me.

Some articles will be more analytical. Others will just be about basketball. The goal isn't to turn every NBA discussion into an economics paper, but to use economic ideas when they help explain why teams, players, and leagues behave the way they do.

About Me

I grew up watching basketball and, at one point, made the unfortunate decision to try playing it myself. That did not go particularly well. So, following the old saying that “those who can’t do, teach,” I've decided to write instead. Basketball has stuck with me even if my jump shot didn't, and my dream is to eventually turn that obsession into a career working in the NBA.

I'm currently an economics student, and The Net Effect is where those two interests come together. I started the site as a place to explore the economics, incentives, strategy, and weird questions behind the NBA, while also giving myself an excuse to write about basketball more generally.

What You'll Find Here

Expect pieces on game theory, salary-cap strategy, player value, competitive balance, team-building decisions, NBA history, and plenty of less serious basketball questions along the way.
