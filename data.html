NBA Superteam Fragility Dataset

2026

The dataset behind my analysis of whether superstar-heavy NBA teams become more vulnerable by concentrating a large share of their value in a small number of players.

It compares six superteam seasons with five comparison contenders between 2012-13 and 2022-23, using measures including title odds, net rating, core-player Win Shares, availability, Net On/Off, and value-weighted games missed.

Workbook includes:
  • Team-season dataset
  • Player-level core data
  • Full methodology and variable definitions
  • Analysis dashboard
  • Source links for the underlying data
